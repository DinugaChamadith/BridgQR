FAQ

Questions, answered

Is BridgQR really free?

Yes — every feature is free and unlimited. There are no accounts, watermarks, or usage caps.

Do my links get stored anywhere?

No. The QR code is generated entirely on your device using JavaScript. Nothing is uploaded, logged, or shared — you can even disconnect from the internet after the page loads.

Which export resolution should I pick?

Use 256–512 px for screens and digital signage. Choose 1024 px for print, where the code may be reproduced at a large physical size.

Will a custom-colored code still scan?

As long as there's strong contrast between the foreground and background — dark modules on a light background works best. Always test-scan before printing at scale.

Can I use the generated codes commercially?

Absolutely. The codes you generate are yours to use anywhere — personal or commercial, with no attribution required.
